# YouTube Release-Safety Reference

Use this reference during the controller's Commercial Safety Gate. Policies change; verify the current official pages before making a high-stakes release decision.

## Official references

- Advertiser-friendly content guidelines: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278
- YouTube channel monetization policies, including reused and inauthentic content: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- Altered or synthetic content disclosure: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- Community Guidelines overview: https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/policies/community-guidelines/

## Gate checks

### Advertiser suitability

- Is graphic injury, death, violence, sexual content, profanity, dangerous behavior, or drug use the focal point?
- Does the title, thumbnail, or opening intensify a sensitive topic beyond the documentary context?
- Can the same thesis be delivered with non-graphic language and imagery?

### Sensitive events

- Is recent suffering being used mainly for traffic?
- Are victims treated with dignity and uncertainty handled carefully?
- Does the video add reporting, analysis, education, or documentary value?

### Originality and reuse

- Does the episode contain substantial original research, narration, analysis, editing, and structure?
- Is third-party material transformed rather than merely compiled?
- Does the channel avoid mass-produced scripts with interchangeable hooks, examples, and conclusions?

### Altered or synthetic media

- Could a realistic generated or altered person, voice, place, event, or recording be mistaken for authentic material?
- If yes, recommend the platform's altered/synthetic disclosure.
- Do not use disclosure as permission to fabricate evidence or impersonate a real person's factual statement.

## Report format

- `status`: green, yellow, or red
- `policy_area`
- `affected_element`
- `reason`
- `required_fix`
- `safer_alternative`
- `synthetic_media_disclosure`: yes, no, or review
